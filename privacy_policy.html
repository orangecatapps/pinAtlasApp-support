Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Pin Atlas ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy.

Data we collect

Pin Atlas does not collect any personal data. All data you enter — including pin photos, team names, and collection details — is stored locally on your device.

Photos

Photos you take or import are processed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. No images are transmitted to external servers.

Club crest images

Club crest images are downloaded from API-Football (api-football.com). No information about you or your collection is sent with these requests, beyond the standard technical information that any internet request includes (such as your IP address).

Purchases

Pin Atlas Pro purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details.

iCloud

If you have iCloud enabled, your collection data may be synced across your Apple devices via Apple's CloudKit infrastructure, subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Third parties

We do not share your data with any third parties.

If you have any questions, contact us at orangecatapps@gmail.com
